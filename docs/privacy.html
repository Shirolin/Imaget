Privacy policy

Last updated 3 April 2026

Imaget does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. All image sniffing, processing and downloading happens inside your browser.

What the extension reads

It reads the page you have open to find images: image tags, srcset lists, CSS backgrounds, inline SVG and nested frames. That work stays in the tab and is never sent anywhere.

Permissions and what they do

  • downloads saves images to your device or packs them into a ZIP.
  • activeTab, http://*/* and https://*/* let the panel find images on the page you visit.
  • storage keeps your filters and preferences on your machine.
  • contextMenus adds the "Download with Imaget" entry to the right-click menu.
  • sidePanel opens the panel beside the page.

Third parties

There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. No data is shared with anyone.

Where the work happens

Finding image URLs, converting formats and packing a ZIP all run on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and there is no account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
