Imaget — Smart Batch Image Downloader & Converter

Tired of right-clicking and saving images one by one? Imaget is here to save the day!


▎ What Can It Do for You?

▶ Deep Integration for Pixiv, Twitter/X, Weibo & Reddit
Automatically parses URLs to sniff out original, high-resolution images rather than low-quality thumbnails. Select → Download, and you are done.

▶ Encrypted Blob Stream Penetration (New 🌟)
Fully supports encrypted Blob assets on platforms like Telegram and Discord. Simply click the image to open the preview, and Imaget instantly sniffs and grabs the highest resolution original image.

▶ Native Sidepanel Support for Right-Click Restricted Sites
Runs elegantly inside Chrome's native sidepanel without interrupting your web browsing. It bypasses right-click restrictions or hotlink-protected sites effortlessly.

▶ One-Click Batch ZIP Export
Dozens of assets on a page? Use Shift-select or drag-to-select to batch download them instantly. Pack them all into a single ZIP file with one click.


▎ Powerful & Intuitive Features

🔍 Real-Time Advanced Filters
Filter images on the fly by min/max width and height, image formats, URL keywords, or exclusion words.

🔄 Smart Format Conversion
Need to convert WebP images to JPG/PNG? Imaget automatically handles conversions during download with customizable quality control.

📜 Auto-Scroll Scanning (Waterfall Flow Savior)
Stuck on a dynamic scrolling page? Click "Scroll Scan" and watch Imaget automatically scroll to the bottom, rendering and extracting all images on the page.

📁 Organized Folders & Smart Rename
Keep your downloads tidy by automatically sorting files into subfolders based on page titles. Customize filename templates to eliminate random filenames.

🖱️ Floating Download Button
Hover over any image to reveal a quick preview/download button. Save images on the go without cluttering your workflow.

🖼️ Lightning-Fast Image Viewer
Includes a built-in high-performance previewer supporting zoom-in/out, drag-and-drop, and rotation.
